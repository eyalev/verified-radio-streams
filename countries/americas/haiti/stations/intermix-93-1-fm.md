**On air 93.1 FM** is read off the station's own name, which is where
the only frequencies in this dataset come from. A broadcaster with transmitters
in several regions has a frequency in each; what is listed is what was written
down, not a complete set.

talk or music. It is not a claim that it is neutral or mixed. Genre tags are
crowd-submitted and are not evidence of speech content, so this project infers
`music` from musical genres and never infers `talk`.
