# Radio, by country

**7,707 stations across 188 countries.** Every stream here was opened
and returned audio before it was listed.

## Browsable here

🇵🇹 **[Portugal](portugal/)**

Country pages inside this repo are being added, starting with the one above. Every other country
below links straight to its page on **[globalradio.app](https://globalradio.app)**, where you can
press play — plus its raw `json` and `m3u`, which exist for all 188.

<sup>†</sup> 16 countries have fewer than 3 verified stations and so get **no page on
the site** — a country page listing one station is not a page. Their stations
are real and are in the `json` and `m3u` here. A small number means we have
not found more that answer, not that the country has little radio.
